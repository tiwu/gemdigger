⛏ Welcome to GemDigger!

  • Move with WASD, Arrow Keys or the D-Pad to dig.
  • 💎 Goal: Dig deep to find gems and earn score!
  • 🏠 Surface Base: Return to the house at the top (y=0) to sell gems, Refuel, Repair, and buy Upgrades!
  • ⛽ Fuel: Fuel drains as you move. Don't run out!
  • 🛡️ Hull: Lava and deep rocks damage your hull. Repair at base!
  • 🚧 Outposts: Buy outpost kits to create refuel points deep underground.

What's New in v3.0.0

⚒️ The Drill Wear Update

  • Soft Locks: You can now drill rock harder than your drill power, but it will damage your Hull!
  • Reinforced Bits: New shop upgrade to reduce damage from tough rocks.
  • Per-Tile Fuel: Fuel consumption now depends on what you're drilling. Empty space is cheap!
  • WASD Support: Use W/A/S/D or Arrow keys.
  • Emoji Graphics: Gems now sparkle with vibrant emojis. 💎✨
  • Procedural Textures: Dirt and stone have new unique textures.
  • Improved UI: Toasts are bigger, and audio now pauses when you switch tabs.
