⛏ Welcome to GemDigger!

  • Move with Arrow Keys or the D-Pad to dig through dirt, stone and gems.
  • ⛽ Fuel drains as you move/dig, and drains faster the deeper you go. 🛡️ Hull takes damage from lava and cave-ins. Run out of either and it's game over!
  • Dig up gems (Gold, Ruby, Emerald, Diamond, Sapphire, Unobtainium) for score. Chain gem hits quickly for a combo multiplier.
  • Each biome (Surface, Cavern, Ice Layer, Magma Layer, Deep Core) has distinct rock colors, gem chances, hazards and music.
  • Return to the 🏠 Surface Base to refuel and spend score on drill upgrades.
  • Buy an 🚧 Outpost Kit at the shop, carry it deep, and deploy it (button or E key) on cleared ground to refuel without the long trip back to the surface. Max 2 outposts per run!
